## Aturan deck

### A. Format consulting: slide padat tapi tidak bikin pusing

1. **Judul = kesimpulan (action title).** Satu kalimat, maksimal 15 kata, yang menyatakan *takeaway*-nya (*"Every safeguard asks the person being tricked"*), bukan label (*"Problem"*). **Kalau juri cuma membaca judul slide 1–15 berurutan, ceritanya harus tetap utuh** (cek bagian *Storyline* di bawah).
2. **Kerangka tetap di setiap slide** (kecuali slide 1, 2, 8, 15):
   - **Tracker** kanan atas, memakai **nama kriteria juri** sesuai urutan presentasi: `Track · USP · PoC · Feasibility · Patentability · Scalability`. Kriteria yang sedang dibahas ditebalkan. Dengan begitu juri selalu tahu kriteria mana yang sedang dijawab dan bisa langsung mencentang di lembar nilainya.
   - **Judul aksi** di kiri atas.
   - **Body maksimal 3 blok** (kolom, tabel, atau diagram). Lebih dari 3 blok = pecah atau pindah ke backup.
   - **Kotak *So what*** satu baris di bawah body, latar hijau tint: kalimat yang harus diingat juri.
   - **Footer**: sumber angka (8–9 pt) dan nomor slide.
3. **Satu titik fokus per slide.** Setiap slide punya satu elemen paling besar atau paling berwarna (angka, diagram, atau baris tabel yang menyala). Mata juri harus mendarat di situ dulu. Elemen inilah yang **ditunjuk** saat bicara (lihat baris *Fokus mata*).
4. **Aturan anti-mumet:**
   - Bullet maksimal 12 kata. Tidak ada paragraf lebih dari 2 baris.
   - Tabel maksimal 6 baris × 4 kolom.
   - Ukuran huruf: judul 24–28 pt, body 12–16 pt, footer 8–9 pt.
   - Satu ikon per ide, tanpa clip-art.
   - Warna punya makna dan konsisten (palet deck v1, dipakai lagi di **v3**): **teal `#18656F` / ink `#10353D` = RONDA**, **emas `#CF933D` = guardian & aksen**, **merah `#C93B3B` = ancaman / korban**, **oranye `#E07B39` = peringatan**, **abu `#5E767B` = pihak lain / konteks**. Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Regular, SemiBold, ExtraBold).
5. **Yang diucapkan ≠ yang tertulis.** Pembicara cukup mengucapkan judul + satu sorotan, lalu lanjut. Detail lainnya untuk dibaca juri dan untuk amunisi Q&A. **Jangan membacakan slide.**
6. **Slide napas.** Slide 1 (pembuka), 2 (hook), 8 (demo), dan 15 (penutup) sengaja **minim teks dan sinematik**. Kontrasnya membuat slide padat terasa wajar, bukan melelahkan.

### B. Aturan isi

7. **Hook = empati, bukan data.** Cerita fiktif boleh, asal ada label kecil *Illustrative story* di slide, dan slide berikutnya langsung membuka bahwa ceritanya fiktif tapi kejadiannya nyata. Ini menjaga kepercayaan juri.
8. **Tutup dengan kembali ke cerita pembuka** (*bookend*).
9. **Tidak ada angka tanpa sumber.** Semua angka ada di Lampiran C. Rencana ke depan ditulis tanpa janji tanggal.
10. **Ikuti kriteria juri.** Setiap slide diberi label **Kriteria** (utama + pendukung). Keenam kriteria punya slide sendiri, dan porsi waktunya mengikuti bobot (lihat tabel *Pembagian waktu per kriteria*). Istilah kriteria juga **diucapkan** di awal bagiannya (*"On feasibility…"*, *"On patentability…"*) supaya juri tidak perlu menebak.

# SLIDE 5 — *"Every safeguard asks the person being tricked."*

**Kriteria:** **Accordance with Track** (utama) · USP (dasar argumen)
**Tracker:** **Track** · USP · PoC · Feasibility · Patentability · Scalability

**Di slide — 3 blok:**

**Blok atas (fokus): alur 4 layar HP kecil**, kiri ke kanan, masing-masing dengan jari yang menekan:
1. Chat: `Undangan Pernikahan.apk` — *"Mohon dibuka ya Bu"*
2. Android: *"Allow from this source"* → toggle
3. Android: *"Do you want to install this app?"* → **Install**
4. App palsu: *"Allow access to SMS?"* → **Allow**

Di bawah keempat layar ada baris merah: **Who decides? → Victim · Victim · Victim · Victim**. Di antara layar 3 dan 4 ada penanda hijau: `RONDA steps in here: after install, before any permission`.

**Blok kiri bawah: why warnings fail**

| # | Alasan | Keterangan |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Too late** | She's trusted the "caller" for 20 minutes; a pop-up can't compete |
| 2 | **Pre-empted** | Scam script: *"a warning will appear, just tap continue"* |
| 3 | **Always behind** | APK repackaged every campaign; signatures lag |

**Blok kanan bawah: track fit**
> **Human-Centric Security:** *"Design a system that changes or influences user behavior to reduce and manage cybersecurity risks."*
> 64% of cyber incidents stem from human error (Kaspersky, 2023)
> **Our answer: don't train the victim. Change who decides.**

**So what:** *The weak point isn't the phone. It's who the phone is asking.*

**Footer sumber:** Kaspersky Human Factor 360 Report 2023 · Guideline HackNusa 2026 (teks track)

**Fokus mata:** baris merah *Victim · Victim · Victim · Victim*.

**Yang diomongin (Dhanes):**
> Android did try to protect her: allow this source? Install? Allow SMS? Every question went to the one person being tricked — often with the scammer on the line saying "just tap continue."
>
> Our track, Human-Centric Security, asks us to change behaviour to reduce risk. We don't make her an expert. We change *who decides*.

**Catatan tim:** RONDA menilai app **tepat setelah instalasi selesai** (`InstallReceiver`, `ACTION_PACKAGE_ADDED`), yaitu sebelum app dibuka dan sebelum izin apa pun diberikan. Teks prompt Android berbeda-beda tergantung merek HP, jadi tulis versi pendek saja.

# SLIDE 6 — *"RONDA moves the decision to someone the scammer can't reach."*

**Kriteria:** **USP** (solution overview)
**Tracker:** Track · **USP** · PoC · Feasibility · Patentability · Scalability

**Di slide — 3 blok:**

**Blok atas (fokus): dua HP + 5 langkah**: HP orang tua (kiri, label hijau *Rondee*) dan HP penjaga (kanan, label biru *Rondor*), dengan 5 langkah berjajar di antaranya:

| Langkah | Apa yang terjadi | Bagaimana (detail teknis kecil) |
|---|---|---|
| **① Connect** | Pair once | 6-letter code read over a call, or QR |
| **② Detect** | Scores every new app, 0–100 | Declared permissions + install source, on-device, offline |
| **③ Block** | Covers the app at every open | Full-screen overlay, works with data off |
| **④ Alert** | Guardian gets plain-language reasons | Realtime via Firebase into the guardian's review queue |
| **⑤ Decide** | Remove it, or mark it safe | Uninstall request to the parent's phone; "safe" has 10-s undo |

**Blok kiri bawah: two roles, two jobs**
- **Parent's phone:** zero security decisions, minimal UI
- **Guardian's phone:** evidence, score and reasons; decides

**Blok kanan bawah: band skor**
`0–29 Safe · 30–59 Low (logged, silent) · 60–89 Warning (block + alert) · 90–100 Danger (block + loud alert)`

**So what:** *RONDA flags the app at install, before a single permission is granted.*

**Fokus mata:** panah ④ Alert dari HP kiri ke HP kanan.

**Yang diomongin (Malik):**
> Here's how it works: one app on two phones, paired once with a six-letter code.
>
> When an app is installed on the parent's phone, RONDA scores it on the device, before any permission is granted. If it's dangerous, it's covered every time it opens, even offline, and the guardian gets the reasons — then decides: remove it, or mark it safe.

# SLIDE 7 — *"In Indonesia, every defence asks the victim. Only RONDA asks a guardian."*

**Kriteria:** **USP**
**Tracker:** Track · **USP** · PoC · Feasibility · Patentability · Scalability

**Di slide — 2 blok:**

**Blok kiri (fokus): tabel kompetitor** (semua baris abu-abu, baris RONDA menyala hijau dengan chip biru *Guardian*):

| Solution | What it does | Blind spot | Who decides? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Play Protect, Enhanced Fraud Protection (ID, Feb 2025) | Blocks sideloads asking SMS / accessibility | Can be switched off; the script pre-empts it | Victim |
| Android developer verification (30 Sep 2026) | Blocks unverified apps from app stores | Chat-sent APKs wait until 2027; verified ≠ safe | Victim |
| Bank apps | Block screen-control abuse; check at transfer | Protects one app; the OTP has already leaked | Victim |
| Telco filters | Filter calls & links in the network | WhatsApp is encrypted; APK never passes the filter | — |
| Family-alert apps (US) | Alert family on scam calls | US-only, call-centric, not in Indonesia | Family |
| **RONDA** | **Scores every install; blocks; alerts** | **—** | **Guardian** |

**Blok kanan: 3 differentiators**
1. **A second human.** Someone the scammer isn't talking to.
2. **Scores, not verdicts.** 0–100 with named reasons: quiet apps stay quiet, so alerts stay credible.
3. **Complements, doesn't compete.** Banks can't see outside their app, telcos can't read WhatsApp, Google doesn't know who your son is.

**So what:** *Google strengthens the door. RONDA changes who opens it.*

**Footer sumber:** Google Indonesia Blog (18 Feb 2025) · Android Developers Blog (Mar & Jun 2026)

**Fokus mata:** kolom *Who decides?*

**Yang diomongin (Malik):**
> That's our USP. Every defence in Indonesia — Google's, the banks', the telcos' — ends with a question on the victim's screen. RONDA is the only one here that brings in a second person the scammer can't reach.
>
> It gives a score, not a yes-or-no, so harmless apps stay quiet. And it doesn't replace Google or the banks — it covers the blind spot each of them has.

**Catatan tim:**
- Baris *Family-alert apps (US)* sengaja ditampilkan: itu bukti model guardian laku dijual. Karena itu klaimnya **"only one in Indonesia"**, bukan "only one in the world". Jangan diubah.
- Pertanyaan yang paling mungkin muncul: *"Google already does this."* Jawabannya ada di Lampiran A #1.

# SLIDE 13 — *"The scanner isn't patentable. The guardian-mediated protocol is."*

**Kriteria:** **Level of its patentability**
**Tracker:** Track · USP · PoC · Feasibility · **Patentability** · Scalability

**Di slide — 3 blok:**

**Blok kiri (fokus): patentability ladder** (tiga baris bertingkat, warna dari abu ke hijau):

| Component | Prior art | Level | We claim it as… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Permission scanner | extensive (antivirus, Play Protect) | **Low** | not claimed |
| Two-axis score + consequence-language reasons | known methods (CVSS-style), new combination | **Medium** | dependent claim |
| **Guardian-mediated intervention protocol** | no known install-time equivalent *(prior-art search pending)* | **High** | **main claim** |

**Blok tengah: the main claim, in one line** (diagram 3 langkah)
> **①** device A scores a newly installed app and **blocks it locally** → **②** the verdict is routed to a **pre-paired human on device B** → **③** B's decision is **executed back on A** (uninstall request or release).

Dependent claims (kecil): install-time scoring of *declared* permissions before any grant · only the guardian can whitelist (asymmetric control) · single-use, expiring pairing code.

**Blok kanan: against Indonesian patent law (UU 13/2016)**

| Test | RONDA |
|---|---|
| **Novelty** (Art. 3) | main claim, subject to prior-art search |
| **Inventive step** (Art. 3) | the industry adds better warnings *to the victim*; moving the decision to a second device is not the obvious step |
| **Industrial applicability** (Art. 3) | licensable to banks, telcos, insurers |
| **Not "only a computer program"** (Art. 4) | technical effect: an app on phone A is blocked and removed by a decision on phone B → computer-implemented invention |

**IP path** (pita bawah): prior-art search (PDKI + Google Patents) → patent / simple-patent filing at DJKI → © software copyright · ™ "RONDA" trademark

**So what:** *We don't patent a better scanner. We patent who gets to decide.*

**Footer:** UU No. 13 Tahun 2016 tentang Paten, Pasal 3, 4, dan 6

**Fokus mata:** baris **High — main claim**.

**Yang diomongin (Alek):**
> On patentability, we're precise. The scanner isn't new, and Indonesian law excludes pure software. What is new is the system: one phone blocks an app, a trusted person on a second phone decides, and that decision is carried back and executed. That's a technical effect, so we'd claim it as a computer-implemented system. Next: a prior-art search, then filing with DJKI.

**Catatan tim:**
- **Jangan klaim "patent pending"**. Belum ada yang didaftarkan.
- **Risiko kebaruan (penting):** UU Paten Pasal 6 hanya memberi masa tenggang 6 bulan untuk pengungkapan di pameran resmi, uji coba penelitian, atau forum ilmiah. Repo GitHub publik dan pitching ini **bisa dianggap pengungkapan publik**. Sebelum klaim "High", konsultasikan ke Sentra KI kampus atau DJKI. Kalau ternyata kebaruan sudah gugur, strateginya jadi *defensive publication* (seperti di project report §5.5): publikasi kami mencegah pihak lain mematenkan mekanismenya. Jawabannya untuk Q&A ada di Lampiran A #18.
- Merek "RONDA": cek dulu di PDKI apakah sudah ada merek serupa di kelas 9/42 sebelum menyebut "trademark" sebagai rencana pasti.
